## Direction contract

THESIS: Hotelghino reads as a live trip-planning command center, not a static brochure — search, manage, and book screens all share one confident, saturated product voice, refusing the muted corporate-blue-on-white SaaS template it currently wears.

OWN-WORLD: Committed indigo-blue (#2A4FE0 refreshed from the flatter #2f5aa8) drives structure, navigation, and primary actions; a warm sunset-coral (#FF6B4A) carries high-energy CTAs and highlights; a teal (#0DB6A6) carries positive/availability status. Full-palette strategy (3-4 named roles) on a crisp near-white ground (#F6F8FC) with ink-navy text (#101A33). Cards float on soft, offset, blurred shadows with generous 16-20px radii; a gradient-mesh wash (indigo → coral) is reserved for the hero only, never repeated as decoration elsewhere. Type: Manrope for display/headings (geometric, confident, distinct from the outgoing Montserrat), Inter for body/UI/tables (the Operate workhorse — dense forms, dashboards, data tables).

STORY: A guest lands on the hero, instantly reads "search and book stays," and acts through a floating, elevated search card. A propietario/admin sees the identical visual language render as a calm, scannable operating console — management cards, status pills, tables — with no loud CTA fighting the task at hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home.html hero): full-bleed gradient-mesh hero (indigo to coral) behind a headline + subcopy on the left; a floating glass-panel search form (destino / fechas / CTA in coral) overlaps the hero's bottom edge as an elevated card breaking out of the section, giving the fold immediate energy and a clear primary action.

FORM: builds the user's own pinned choice directly — "plataforma tech moderna" (Airbnb/Booking-caliber product feel with its own identity), light theme, blue kept as the primary brand color, existing logo untouched — captured through the structured question round in this session rather than the concept-seed dice; a user-pinned direction beats the roll, so no seed key applies here.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
